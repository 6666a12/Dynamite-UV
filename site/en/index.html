01 / PROJECT

The game and the editor

PLAY / 01

Dynamite Universe

Search by title, artist or chart author. Preview a song and pick a difficulty. You can play all three lanes with a mouse or multitouch. Calibrate timing and adjust note speed and colors in the settings. Use Mirror to flip the layout or watch the chart in Auto without recording a score.

Hardcore tightens the timing windows. Turn on BLEED if you want to play with a health bar. The results screen shows the judgment counts. The game keeps separate local bests for Standard and Hardcore. Sign in to browse a server's chart store. If you finish a chart you downloaded from that server, the game tries to upload your score.

Before you share a chart

Check that you have permission to distribute the music and cover art with your pack. You can use the guide's click track for practice.

Dynamite Universe is an unofficial reimplementation made by the community. Public builds contain none of the original game's assets or charts. The repository has no project-wide open-source license; public access to the code does not grant redistribution rights.
