01 / PROJECT

The game and the editor

PLAY / 01

Dynamite Universe

Search by title, artist or chart author. Listen to a preview, pick a difficulty and play the left, center and right lanes with a mouse or multitouch. Calibrate timing, adjust note speed or colors, play with Mirror, or watch in Auto. Auto runs don't count toward your scores.

Pick Hardcore for tighter timing windows or turn on BLEED to play with a health bar. The results screen breaks down your judgments. Standard and Hardcore keep separate local bests. Sign in to browse a server's chart store. When you finish playing a chart you downloaded from that server, the game tries to upload your score.

03 / SOFTWARE

Open the editor

If you have the Windows test build, extract the whole archive and run DynaMakerUV.exe. If there's no public download on the releases page, you can run the editor from source.

Before you share a chart

Check that you have permission to distribute the music and cover art with your pack. You can use the guide's click track for practice.

Dynamite Universe is an independent community reimplementation, not an official release of the original game. Public builds exclude its assets and charts. The repository has no project-wide open-source license; public access to the code does not grant redistribution rights.
